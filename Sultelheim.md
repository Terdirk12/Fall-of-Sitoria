Sultelheim is a adventurers village, created to support adventurers venturing into [[Ruined Edelheim]] or [[The Malian Republic]]

The village has multiple competing blacksmiths offering discounts on various items:

| Blacksmith                      | Speciality              |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Trusty Eric (Human)             | Spears, Swords, Shields |
| Sir Gellard (Elf)               | Ranged Weaponry         |
| Thumblebrok Irohnhammer (Dwarf) | Heavy Armor, Hammer     |
