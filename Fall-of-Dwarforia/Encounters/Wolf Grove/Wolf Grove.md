A couple of wolves surround an open ruin with a chest in it, the wolves don't appear to be hostile, but they seem wary, guarding of the chest in the midst of them
- If the player opens the chest, the wolves turn hostile, players roll initiative
- If the player doesn't open the chest, the wolves do not aggro and they can even tame one. (If they picked up the food at the start of the game)
	- The wolve has a name tag with the name Ponyo on it.
	- If Ponyo is tamed (the alpha), they will allow the chest being opened without attacking you
	- Ponyo is a companion and can be carried into combat, see his stat block here:
		- He cannot be ridden, he doesn't like that.
