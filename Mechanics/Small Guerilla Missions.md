Basically endlessly available missions that the players can do across sitoria to gain exp, loot and money.

Add 1 to any mission:
- a rival rebel cell is already on-site
- the target is **secretly sympathetic**
- a time limit: convoy arriving at dawn
- collateral risk: hostages, famine, plague
- the barony wants **plausible deniability**
- the emperor’s agent is hunting the same target

---
**Bandit Groups**
the players can deal with bandits by:
- positive takeover, kill a cruel bandit leader to let their softer second in command take over.
- turn bandits to rebels, get more recruits by ways of diplomacy.
- locate smuggling routes, to benefit rebel movements.
- stop fake collections, bandits posing as rebels collecting taxes.

---
**Mercenary Groups**
players can deal with mercenaries by:
- Loot Mercenary Supplies, get supplies for the rebels.
- Intercept payments, without pay they will likely leave.
- Turn mercenary captain, recruit a mercenary captain to the rebellion.
- theater relocation, inform mercenaries that a different location pays better.


---
**Goblin Wars**
The players can invoke wars by doing:
- False-flag raids, Steal goods from clan A and dump them at clan B
- Assassinating leaders, Kill a goblin leader and plant evidence of another clan
- Burning goblin goods, Sabotaging Go-Juice production and blame other clans
- Empowering lower-ranked clans, Making smaller clans stronger so they can be a threat to bigger clans.

---
**Explore**
Players are a sort of forward scouting group for the rebellion, they can improve the rebellions odds by doing recon and scouting missions:
- Survey hostile fort, take notes of a hostile fortress to improve odds of capture.
- Study monster ecology movement, perhaps lead hostile lifeforms to the empire.
- Plot safehouses, find suitable locations in which rebel groups can hide after a loss.
- Resource allocation, find locations the rebellion could use to improve.

depending on the type of area they explore different random events may happen.
