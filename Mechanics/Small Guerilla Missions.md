**Goblin Wars**
The players can invoke wars by doing:
- False-flag raids, Steal goods from clan A and dump them at clan B
- Assassinating leaders, Kill a goblin leader and plant evidence of another clan
- Burning goblin goods, Sabotaging Go-Juice production and blame other clans
- Empowering lower-ranked clans, Making smaller clans stronger so they can be a threat to bigger clans.
