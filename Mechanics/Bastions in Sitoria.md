# Types
There are three bastion types:
Castles: Castles are a long-form Bastion, they take ages to build but with the right management centuries to tear down. 
Castles come with some basic upgrades, like Walls, Watchtower, Hunters and a storage room.
A Castle has 3 A slot(s) and 6 B slot(s)

Forts: Forts are strategic Bastions, from a fort a region can be held secure, using its garrison to deal with threats.
Forts come with some basic upgrades, like Walls and a Watchtower. But don't get the foraging perk from the Encampment.
A Fort has 2 A slot(s) and 4 B slot(s)

Encampments: Encampments are movable Bastions, made using an 'encampment kit' which needs to be prepared beforehand. Encampments can be used as a base to assault an enemy position or a safe haven whilst exploring.
Encampments get a special perk, *Units in encampments will take care of their own food by foraging.* this is because the presence of an encampment is very small and as such doesn't disturb the normal fauna and flora.
An Encampment has 1 A slot(s) and 2 B slot(s)

Encampment kits can be made by certain crafters in the rebellion, each kit costs 1.000 supplies and 200 gold and takes two days to make.

# Tiers
Encampments can be up tiered into Forts using a high amount of resources.
Forts can be up tiered into Castles using a high amount of resources.

---

# Resources
There are five types of resources in the campaign.
Supplies - Supplies consist of general building supplies, think Wood, Stone, Glass, Leather, Metal.

Gold - Bastions use Gold to purchase supplies, food, recruit new units, garner influence.

Influence - Influence represents political power, reputation and regional control. Influence is gained through quests, trade routes, military victories and helping settlements. Influence is used to recruit special units, establish trade routes and maintain control over conquered regions. 

Exotic - Blueprints, Magical items, Rare materials. Exotic materials are things that unlock special buildings, they are very uncommon and need to be tracked clearly.

Food - Used to feed troops, without this they will starve until the upkeep is lower then the income.

# Bastion Buildings
There are two types of bastion buildings:
Basic - B
Advanced - A

They work in a 2-1 ratio.
If a bastion has 1 A 2 B, players can decide to build 2 A buildings by using both B slots, or 4 B buildings by using the A slot for B buildings
