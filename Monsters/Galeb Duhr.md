### Traits: 
**False Appearance.** while the Galeb Duhr remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a normal boulder.

**Rolling Charge.** If the Galeb Duhr rolls at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a slam attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
___
### Actions:
**Slam.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
_Hit:_ 12 _(2d6 + 5)_ Bludgeoning damage.

**Animate Boulders (1/Day).** The Galeb Duhr magically animates up to two boulders it can see within 60 feet of it. A boulder has statistics like those of a Galeb Duhr, except it has Intelligence 1 and Charisma 1, it cant be charmed or frightened, and it lacks this action option. A boulder remains animated as long as the Galeb Duhr maintains concentration, up to 1 minute (as if concentrating on a spell.)
