Vaelessa used to be a quiet kid, an introvert preferring a quiet night over a ball, except when with her father on which she could always lean and her brother [[Prince Lucerin Vaelic]] who wasnt afraid to distract nobles so she could sneak back to her room. Vaelessa often kept pets as company, always nursing injured lizards, drakes and snakes back to health, in these moments her bright intellect shined most as she seemed to have intricate knowledge about anatomy, cures and remedies. Vaelessa was naturally stealthy always sneaking out of busy places, balls and courts, at night she often snuck into the kitchen to snack, sneaking through the palace to avoid getting a lecture from [[Prince Garran Vaelic]] or [[Princess Ravena Vaelic]]. She often read books with [[Princess Silena Vaelic]] until she left for the monastery, their discussions about the plot, character or writing style of the books could last till deep in the night. Vaelessa used to help her dad with anything animal related, be it which circus to book for the empires festivals, what policies affected cattle farmers and how to keep a relatively large amount of mounts for the army.

On [[The Severing Night]] she was reading in her room about dragons and drakes only to be shook by the sounding of the palace bells, when she ran out and found her dad he was already sobbing over [[Prince Garran Vaelic]]'s corpse, when a messenger ran in with the dire news that [[Prince Lucerin Vaelic]] has also been killed Vaelessa fainted. She would not awaken for three weeks, her dad sobbing quietly at her bed until she woke up.

After [[The Severing Night]] she stayed with her dad trying to keep up his spirits, when [[Blink]] came in to teach her dad about ancient rituals Vaelessa paid close attention and devoted herself to study these rituals to help her dad. She made it her mission to help her dad resurrect her brothers.

Using these rituals Vaelessa became stronger herself, a necessity as some of the material components were found in far corners of the empire sometimes in goblin or rebel held territory. Rebels that encountered her call her inhuman, speaking of ghosts and specters about how she glows an earie glow at night and how arrows seem to pass through her unbothered, they speak of her lightning speed and her cursed magic. one rebel recalls: *"It was just me and Jim left, within an instant she stood before Jim and kissed her, but when the kiss seemed to last longer and longer Jim started to look older, weak like she sucked the life out of him, I ran as hard as I could and all I heard behind me was this earie laugh echoing throughout the night."* Most rebels now avoid anything that resembles a ghost out of fear.

In a particular encounter a couple of rebel inquisitors and priests engaged Vaelessa and thought to have ended her, she was spotted two weeks later killing all those inquisitors in a pub. Leaving a cold blue fire as she left.

Vaelessa's mental health has declined since [[The Severing Night]], she used to avoid busy area's now she just kills people when there are too many. Everyday she tries to feel her dads warmth yet she finds none, he as broken as she is.

For the pc's Vaelessa will show up at every major event but retreat when the empire starts losing, the pc's will fight her atleast twice, once before the gates to the capitol and once in the throne room before the emperor. But she can still be used as a plot element throughout the campaign.

# **VAELESSA VAELIC — Player-Facing Appearance Description**

Vaelessa looks like the ghost of the girl she once was.

She is small and slight, standing with a quiet, inward posture — shoulders slightly drawn, chin low — as if she’s trying to fold herself out of the world. Yet even like this, she radiates a **terrible presence**, the sense that if she stopped holding herself back for even a moment, the air itself would tighten.

Her skin is **eerily white**, pale enough to reflect the moonlight with a faint glow, as if necromancy has washed all warmth from her. From a distance, she almost doesn’t look solid — like a figure caught halfway between life and memory.

Her face is delicate and hollowed, but it’s her eyes that command everything.  
They are **sunken, dark pits**, each ringed in bruised shadows, yet burning deep inside with a fierce, unnatural light — gold like dying lantern flames or molten embers swallowed by night. It feels as though those eyes **stare straight into a person’s secrets**, reading their doubts, their fears, their guilt.

Her hair falls long and white, with faint **tips of gold** betraying the blonde she used to have. When still, it hangs like pale silk. In motion, it trails behind her like smoke.

She wears a suit of **custom imperial armor**, fitted to her slight frame. It bears the sigil of the empire across the chest, but the rest has been reshaped by ritual and madness:  
etched plates with carved runes, pale bone inlays, small skulls fixed into the pauldrons or clasps — some metal, some very real. You get the impression she carries **more bone than necessary**, as if her armor could rearrange itself when called upon.

When she moves, her steps make **no sound at all**.  
When she stands still, the world feels too quiet around her.

And when she smiles — that thin, sad, knowing little smile — something inside you wants to run.

# Vaelessa first encounter Text

_(Read this as Vaelessa steps into view. She does not walk into the scene — she simply _appears_, as though the moonlight condensed into her shape.)_

The moon hangs full and heavy overhead, silver light spilling across the ruined road.  
And then—  
something moves where no footsteps sounded.

A small, pale figure stands at the edge of the light.

Vaelessa.

Her skin glows faintly, like frost catching fire. Her eyes are sunken pits lit with thin lines of molten gold, shifting like something trapped beneath the surface. Her long white hair drifts in the windless night, gold-tipped strands shimmering like dying embers.

When she speaks, her voice is soft.  
Too soft.

> **“The moon is kind tonight.  
> She remembers things… even when I can’t.”**

She tilts her head slightly, listening to some distant echo only she can hear.

> **“I remember running.  
> Bells.  
> Father’s voice—broken.  
> That’s all.  
> After that… it’s just pieces.”**

Her eyes drift across the group, unfocused at first, then lock suddenly onto **Peter III**.  
The golden light inside them brightens.

> **“You know the missing pieces, don’t you?”**

She takes one, silent step forward.

> **“Lórethil.”**  
> She breathes the name like a sigh.  
> **“She died afraid… but not alone.”**

Another step.  
Not aggressive — just _closer than she should be_.

> **“And Igina… poor Igina.  
> She still clings to you.  
> Like a shadow caught on your heart.”**

Her voice trembles, not with cruelty, but with longing.

> **“I lost everyone in one night.  
> You lost them one by one.  
> Which is worse, Peter?”**

Her expression flickers — sadness, confusion, something sharp and feral around the edges.

> **“Sometimes I wonder if I’m still me.  
> Or just… the grief that was left behind.”**

She looks up at the full moon, and for a moment she seems impossibly fragile.

> **“Under this light… I almost feel warm again.”**

Her hand brushes the bone-inlaid armor on her chest. The bones twitch—almost imperceptibly—like something inside them is waking.

Then her voice softens further, nearly breaking:

> **“Please. Don’t take this from me.  
> Don’t take Father’s hope.  
> I can’t lose anything more.”**

She lowers her gaze, and the golden fire in her eyes sharpens into purpose.

> **“If you turn back now… I will forget you were here.  
> I will forget everything.”**

A beat.  
Her tone shifts — still gentle, still broken, but filled with dreadful certainty.

> **“But if you step toward the capital…”**

The air around her chills.  
Her silhouette flickers in the moonlight like a dying flame.

> **“…then I will break you.  
> Even if it breaks me again.”**

Her pupils constrict to pinpoints of gold.  
Her voice becomes barely more than a whisper:

> **“Peter… please.  
> Don’t make me remember more pain.”**

Then she smiles — a sad, haunted, utterly devastated smile.

> **“I’m sorry.”**

Her bones rattle softly, moving under her armor.

> **“I’ll try to make it quick.”**

And the fight begins.
