emperor whatshisfacefromwhocares

original leader of [[sitoria]]

Once a gentle, cautious, well-meaning ruler, widely seen as a “placeholder emperor” awaiting his sons’ reign.  
After [[The Severing Night]], he broke completely and turned to necromancy under the guidance of a mysterious stranger.  
Now obsessed with resurrecting his sons, he rules through grief, ritual, and madness, letting the empire decay around him.
