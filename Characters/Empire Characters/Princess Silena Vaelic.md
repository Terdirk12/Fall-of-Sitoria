## **SILENA VAELIC — Player-Facing Appearance Description**

Silena Vaelic looks nothing like an imperial princess.

She is tall and slender, her posture straight but never proud, as if she’s learned to make herself smaller without bending. Her movements are slow and deliberate, each step placed with care, like someone who has learned that rushing only leads to regret.

Her face is pale and thoughtful, marked by soft lines of exhaustion rather than age. There is intelligence there — sharp, observant — but it’s muted by something heavier. Her eyes are a calm, muted grey, steady and unflinching, yet they carry the look of someone who has seen a tragedy and decided never to look away again. When she meets your gaze, it feels like being **understood without being forgiven**.

Her hair is kept long but simply bound, a faded blonde threaded with early silver, worn loose except when she works. There is no ornamentation, no vanity — only practicality and ritual.

She wears the robes of the monastery: layered whites and greys, clean but well-worn. The fabric bears faint stitchwork of old imperial patterns, carefully unpicked and resewn into sacred symbols, as if her past was deliberately dismantled and repurposed rather than discarded. Around her neck hangs a simple wooden prayer token, smoothed by years of use.

Her hands are roughened, scarred from labor and long hours of service. One leg bears a faint, old limp — barely noticeable unless you watch her walk — the last reminder of a childhood wound that never fully healed.

When she speaks, her voice is soft and even, never raised, never hurried. There is no anger in it, no warmth either — only quiet certainty. She does not try to persuade or command. She states truths as if they are already settled matters.

Standing near her, you feel an odd stillness. The air seems calmer, heavier somehow, as though the world is holding its breath. Even the mention of death feels less threatening in her presence — not because it has been defeated, but because it has been **acknowledged**.

She does not look like someone waiting to be saved.

She looks like someone who has already chosen to stay.
