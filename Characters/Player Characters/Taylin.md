Taylin grew up in a small community with small but sturdy wooden houses surrounded by nature, in the region of .... forests with thick trees and big lakes dotted the landscape. Life was everywhere around her.

Being doted on by her mother Lorelei and her father Tayron with food aplenty and living near many magical creatures in the wilds, made her childhood a happy one. And one full of interest and wonder.

However not all good things are made to last. Lorelei and Tayron worshipped Malar, God of the Hunt. And he does not tolerate weakness.

Taylin has a chronic illness, this means she requires a daily herbal infusion in order to maintain her normal strength.

This did not sit well with Malar and he commanded Layron and Lorelei to “hunt” their daughter who was 15 at the time.

The parents, being consumed by the bloodlust of the god Malar, hunted Taylin. However as the power granted by the Fey wilds allowed her to step with silver grace, she managed to flee.

For the next 2 years Taylin spent her days in Dartmore. A sizable human village with more people than she has ever seen. But simply “being” in the village does not make one a villager. She was seen as an outcast and the villagers paid her no mind. Only Helias, a short and old librarian, seemed to see her distress.

Taylin studied the way of the druid under Helias. This gave her a sense of fulfillment, but not purpose.

There was one other aspect of her life. In the village she encountered a human with a similar build to hers, she is the daughter of a blacksmith and is very fond of fancy daggers. In warfare however her preference lies with a greatsword, as she is a fierce warrior. Her name is Rylka.

They bonded over good food and shared ideals. They both want to change the rules of the world, bring balance and order to a hostile world.

Together they’ve taken on quests, helped many people and vanquished even more foes.

But disaster struck again.

As the human village was attacked by a major drug lord that announced himself as Dotalas. They defended well, but Rylka got captured during the battle.

And Helias was killed.

Confused and filled with loss, two thoughts arose.

Find Rylka and dismantle the organization that killed Helias.
