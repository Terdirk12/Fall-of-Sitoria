---
### Traits:
**Pack Tactics:** The Latchdrake has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one ally is within 5 ft. of the target.

**Undying Instability:** When reduced to 0 HP, the Latchdrake falls prone but does not die. It becomes unstable for 1 turn, unable to move or take actions. At the start of its next turn (or if forcibly removed from a latch), it explodes (see _Explosive Death_).

**Stealthy Swarm:** The Latchdrake can attempt to hide even when only lightly obscured.

---
### Actions:
**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d4 piercing + 1d4 necrotic damage. If latched, target takes 1d4 necrotic damage at the start of its turn.

**Latch (Recharge 5–6).** The Latchdrake can attach itself to a Medium or smaller creature within reach. While latched, the Latchdrake moves with the target. The target can attempt a DC 12 Strength check to shake the Latchdrake off using its action. Latched targets take necrotic damage at the start of each of their turns (1d4).

**Explosive Death.** Triggered when the Latchdrake becomes unstable or is commanded by a Vaelanecrotic speaker: The Latchdrake explodes, dealing 2d6 necrotic damage to creatures within 5 ft. Dex save DC 12 for half damage. Creatures failing the save are also **dazed until the end of their next turn**.
