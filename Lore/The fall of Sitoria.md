[[Sitoria]] was once a land of wealth and stability, ruled for generations by a proud imperial bloodline. Its current emperor was a man of vision and power, with two sons destined to inherit his throne. Both were bound for political marriages — unions meant to secure lasting alliances with powerful neighboring realms.

But ambition can rot even the noblest heart. The princes despised one another, each scheming to seize the empire for himself. On a night now etched into every Sitorian’s memory — [[The Severing Night]] — both struck, and both fell. By dawn, the heirs were dead, their bloodlines ended, and the royal marriages — along with the alliances they promised — were forever severed.

The emperor withdrew from the world, and the realm began to fracture. Neighboring kingdoms claimed borderlands, bandits ruled the roads, and goblin tribes carved out territories in the chaos.

From the ruins, a new force has risen: [[Sitoria United]], a rebel movement seeking to depose the absent emperor, restore order, and reclaim the empire’s lost lands.

You have fought for their cause before. Now, with sword, spell, and will, you will decide Sitoria’s fate.
