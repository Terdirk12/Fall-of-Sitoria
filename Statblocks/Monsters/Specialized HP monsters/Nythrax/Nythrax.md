### Traits:
**Temperature Vision:** The Nythrax does not suffer from disadvantages when its target is obscured by heat or cold.

---
### Legendary Actions:
**Superheat:** The Nythrax superheats the surrounding waters causing lower visibility and an upward draft that the Nythrax uses to hover. 

---
### Actions:
**Multi attack:** The Nythrax makes two actions per turn of the available list below

**Bite** 
_The Nythrax tries to have a little nibble._
_One Target_ | 10 ft Reach | +7 to Hit | 2d6 Bludgeoning + 3 & 2d6 Lightning Damage | 5ft push.

**Roar** 
_The Nythrax Lets out a loud roar._
_Multiple Targets_ | 20 ft Sphere | Deafen & 2d8 Thunder Damage. 

**Tail Slam** 
_The Nythrax swipes it's tail at you._
_Multiple Targets_ | 15 ft Radius | DC 15 Dex | 3d6 Bludgeoning + 3 | 
On Save: Half Damage
On Fail: Prone

**Grab Plateau** 3-6
_The Nythrax Dissapears underwater... The platform (PC) is standing on begins to shake and shake_ 
**DC 15 Str check. Advantage if prone**
On Fail: Shaken off into the water.
On Save: Immobilized until the end of your next turn.

**Hypnotic Waves** 4-6
_The Nythrax rises out of the water looking at you, it opens its hood and you see a hypnotizing pattern on the inside_ 
All that can see the inside of the hood need to make an **Wis 15 Saving throw**. 
On Fail: Restrained

**Slithering Grasp** 3-6
_The Nythrax rapidly slides towards you and tries to curl around you._
**DC 15 Atlethics/Acrobatics Saving throw**.
On Fail: Grappled
On Save: Not Grappled

3 damage per turn for grappled creature.

**Serpentine Charge** 5-6
_The Nythrax slithers towards you at high speed._
Charges in a 50ft Line | DC 15 Dex Save | 2d8 Bludgeoning + 3 & 1d12 Lightning Damage.
On Fail: + 1d8 Slashing damage
On Save: No slashing damage.

**Swallow** 6-6
_The Nythrax opens it giant Maw and: (Scoops you out of the water.)/(Clamps down on you.)_

DC 15 Str save | _One large or smaller creature either Grappled by the Nythrax or in the water._
On Fail: Target is Swallowed and the Grappled condition is replaced by the Restrained and Blinded Conditions.

The target has total cover against attacks and other effects outside the Nythrax. They take 4d8 Acid damage at the start of the Nythrax's turn.

If the Nythrax takes 20 damage or more from the inside in a **singular** turn it needs to make a DC 20 Con Saving throw or they regurgitate all Swallowed Creatures which fall prone.

### Super Heated Attacks:

**Tail Swipe** 
_The Nythrax swipes its tail at you from above._
_One Target_ | 15 ft Reach | +7 to Hit | 4d6 Bludgeoning + 3. | 10ft push.

**Hot Bite** 
_The Nythrax tries to have a little nibble._
_One Target_ | 10 ft Reach | +7 to Hit | 2d6 Bludgeoning + 3 & 2d6 Fire Damage | 5ft push.

**Steam Breath** 2-6
_The Nythrax opens its jaw and releases a cloud of hot steam into the area._
_Multiple Targets_ | 60 ft Line | DC 15 Dex | 1d6 Bludgeoning + 3 & 3d6 Fire Damage | On Save: Half Damage

**Hot Tornadoes** 2-6
_The Nythrax breathes 3 plumes of hot steam into the area._

Summons three 5ft tornadoes, the tornadoes grow 5ft per turn to a max of 15ft.
A creature inside the tornado takes 4d8 Fire damage.

**Steam of the ages** 
_The Nythrax releases an excessive amount of steam into the room_ 
all creatures in the room are heavily obscured by the steam

**Hypnotic Room** 5-6
_The Nythrax's hood starts glowing and the entire room is glistening in a Hypnotic pattern._ 
All that can see make a **Wis 15 Saving throw**. 
On Fail: Restrained

**Gushing Wind** 3-6
_The Nythrax's hood starts glowing and the entire room is glistening in a Hypnotic pattern._ 
_Multiple Targets_ | 60 ft Wide, 60 ft High, 120ft Long Line | DC 12 Dex | 1d6 fire Damage | 
On Save: Stay
On Fail: 20ft push back
Moving against the wind takes double movement.

**Fire Cracker** 6-6
_Multiple Targets_ | Room long cone | No Save |
The Nythrax Activates this attack at the end of its turn, it will only come into action at the start of its next turn. This allows the players one turn to move or deal damage.

Every 30 damage dealt to the Nythrax will make it wiggle shaking loose a rock from the ceiling for the players to hide behind.

18d6 fire/lightning Damage | all rocks are destroyed.
