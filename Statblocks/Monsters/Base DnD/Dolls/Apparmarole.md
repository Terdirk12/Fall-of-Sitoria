### Traits:
**Antimagic Susceptibility.** The apparmarole has the [Incapacitated](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#incapacitated_xphb) condition while in the area of an [Antimagic Field](https://5e.tools/spells.html#antimagic%20field_xphb) spell. If targeted by a [Dispel Magic](https://5e.tools/spells.html#dispel%20magic_xphb) spell, the apparmarole must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against the caster's spell save DC or have the [Unconscious](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#unconscious_xphb) condition for 1 minute.

**Damage Transfer.** While grappling a creature, the apparmarole has [Resistance](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#resistance_xphb) to all damage, and the creature [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb) by the apparmarole takes damage equal to that taken by the apparmarole.

**False Appearance.** While the apparmarole remains motionless and isn't flying, it is indistinguishable from normal clothing.

### Actions:
**Constrict**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ If the target is Medium or smaller, it has the [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb) condition (escape DC 12). While [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb), it has the [Restrained](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_xphb) and [Blinded](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#blinded_xphb) conditions, and takes 4 (1d4 + 2) Bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns. The apparmarole can grapple one creature at a time.

**Puppeteer**. _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 13, one creature [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb) by the apparmarole. _Failure:_ The target has the [Charmed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#charmed_xphb) condition for 8 hours. While [Charmed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#charmed_xphb), the target wears the apparmarole, shares a space with it, and loses the [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb) condition. The apparmarole and the target have a telepathic link, and the apparmarole can issue the target commands (no action required), which the target does its best to obey.

Each time the target takes damage, it repeats the save. On a success, it ends the effect and pushes the apparmarole into the nearest unoccupied space.
