### Traits:
**Immutable Form.** Marie O'Nett can't shape-shift.

**On Strings.** Marie O'Nett can [High Jump](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#high%20jump_xphb) up to 30 feet. It takes no falling damage when descending from this jump.

**Spellbinding Movement.** When Marie O'Nett moves, it can choose one creature within 30 feet that can see it, subjecting the creature to the following effect. _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 11. _Failure:_ 3 (1d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the [Charmed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#charmed_xphb) condition until the end of Marie O'Nett's next turn or until it takes damage. While [Charmed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#charmed_xphb), the target's [Speed](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#speed_xphb) is halved.
