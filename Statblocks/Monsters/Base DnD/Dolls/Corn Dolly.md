### Traits:
**Husk Body**. The corn dolly can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.

**Uneasy Visage**. The strange, childlike inked face unnerves onlookers. While a creature is holding the corn dolly, creatures within 10 feet of it have [Disadvantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#disadvantage_xphb) on Wisdom (Insight) checks made against the creature.

---
### Actions:

**Husk Cut**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 1 Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Psychic damage.

**Blank Stare.** _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 13, one creature the corn dolly can see within 30 feet. _Failure:_ 10 (3d6) Psychic damage. _Success:_ Half damage.

**Sowing Dread** (2/Day). _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 13, each creature of the corn dolly's choice in a 15-foot [Cone](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#cone%20%5barea%20of%20effect%5d_xphb). _Failure:_ The target has the [Frightened](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#frightened_xphb) condition until the end of its next turn.

### Reactions:
**Silent Protector** (Recharge after a Short or Long Rest). _Trigger:_ A creature within 60 feet fails a saving throw against an effect that doesn't deal damage. _Response:_ The corn dolly suffers the effects of the failure instead of the creature.
