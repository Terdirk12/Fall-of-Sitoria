**Immutable Form.** Dolly can't shape-shift.

### Actions:

**Multiattack**. Dolly uses Creepy Voice and makes one Touch attack.

**Touch**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +4, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 5 (2d4) Psychic damage. _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 11. _Failure:_ The target has the [Restrained](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_xphb) condition until the end of Dolly's next turn.

**Creepy Voice**. _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 11, each creature in a 15-foot [Emanation](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#emanation%20%5barea%20of%20effect%5d_xphb) originating from Dolly that can hear it. _Failure:_ 5 (2d4) Psychic damage, and the target has the [Frightened](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#frightened_xphb) condition until the end of Dolly's next turn. _Success:_ The target is immune to becoming [Frightened](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#frightened_xphb) by Dolly's Creepy Voice for 1 hour, but can still take damage.
