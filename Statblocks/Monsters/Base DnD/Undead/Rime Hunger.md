### Traits:
**Fed Fortitude**. For 1 minute after the rime hunger regains [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb), when damage reduces the rime hunger to 0 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb), it can make a Constitution saving throw (DC 5 plus the damage taken), unless the damage is Fire, Radiant, or from a [Critical Hit](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#critical%20hit_xphb). On a successful save, the rime hunger drops to 1 [Hit Point](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) instead.
