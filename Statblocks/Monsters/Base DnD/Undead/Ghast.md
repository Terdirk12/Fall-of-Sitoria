### Traits:
**Stench:** Any creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of the ghast must make a DC 10 Con saving throw or be poisoned at the start of its next turn. On a successful saving throw the creature is immune to the ghast's stench for 24 hours.

**Turning Defiance:** The ghast and any ghouls within 30 feet of it have advantage on saving throws that turn undead.

Claws.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target  
_Hit:_ 7 (2d4 + 2) Slashing damage. If the target is a creature other then an elf or undead, it must succeed on a DC10 Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 minute. The target can repeat its saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
