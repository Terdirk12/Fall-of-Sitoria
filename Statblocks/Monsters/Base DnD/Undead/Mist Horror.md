### Traits:
**Air Form**. The mist horror can enter an enemy's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.

**One with the Mists**. While the mist horror is in fog or mist, it has the [Invisible](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#invisible_xphb) condition. The mist horror has the [Incapacitated](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#incapacitated_xphb) condition while it is outside an area of fog or mist

---
### Actions:
**Multiattack**. The mist horror makes two Mind Rend attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting to cast [Command](https://5e.tools/spells.html#command_xphb) if available.

**Mind Rend.** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 8 (1d10 + 3) Psychic damage.

**Spellcasting**. The mist horror casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):

- At will: [Fog Cloud](https://5e.tools/spells.html#fog%20cloud_xphb)
- 1/day: [Command](https://5e.tools/spells.html#command_xphb)

---
### Bonus actions:
**Instill Dread** (Recharge 4–6). _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 13, each creature in the mist horror's space. _Failure:_ The target has the [Frightened](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#frightened_xphb) condition until the start of the mist horror's next turn.
