### Traits:
**Regeneration**. The vampire regains 10 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 [Hit Point](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) and isn't in sunlight or running water. If the vampire takes Radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of the vampire's next turn.

**Spider Climb.** The vampire can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

**Vampire Weaknesses.** The vampire has the traits described in the "Vampire Weaknesses" sidebar in the Monsters of Drakkenheim.

---
### Actions:
**Multiattack**. The vampire makes two attacks and uses Feast, if available. It can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting.

**Blood Drain.** _Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:_ +7, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. _Hit:_ 21 (5d6 + 4) Necrotic damage, and the vampire regains [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) equal to the damage dealt. The target has the [Weakened](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#weakened_monstersofdrakkenheim) condition (DC 15 Constitution save ends).

**Feast** (Recharge 5–6). _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 15, each creature in a 30-foot [Emanation](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#emanation%20%5barea%20of%20effect%5d_xphb) originating from the vampire. _Failure:_ 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage plus 14 (4d6) Psychic damage. _Success:_ Half damage. _Failure or Success:_ The vampire regains [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.

**Spellcasting**. The vampire casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15, +7 to spell attacks):

- At will: [Mage Armor](https://5e.tools/spells.html#mage%20armor_xphb) (included above)
- 2/day each: [Darkness](https://5e.tools/spells.html#darkness_xphb), [Fear](https://5e.tools/spells.html#fear_xphb), [Hold Person](https://5e.tools/spells.html#hold%20person_xphb), [Inflict Wounds](https://5e.tools/spells.html#inflict%20wounds_xphb), [Ray of Enfeeblement](https://5e.tools/spells.html#ray%20of%20enfeeblement_xphb), [Suggestion](https://5e.tools/spells.html#suggestion_xphb)
- 1/day: [Bestow Curse](https://5e.tools/spells.html#bestow%20curse_xphb), [Dominate Person](https://5e.tools/spells.html#dominate%20person_xphb), [Hypnotic Pattern](https://5e.tools/spells.html#hypnotic%20pattern_xphb), [Vampiric Touch](https://5e.tools/spells.html#vampiric%20touch_xphb)
