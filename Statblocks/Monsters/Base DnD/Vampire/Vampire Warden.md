### Traits:
**Magic Resistance.** The vampire has [Advantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#advantage_xphb) on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Vampire Weakness.** The vampire has these weaknesses:

- Forbiddance. The vampire can't enter a residence without an invitation from an occupant.
- Running Water. The vampire takes 20 Acid damage if it ends its turn in running water.
- Stake to the Heart. If a weapon that deals Piercing damage is driven into the vampire's heart while the vampire has the [Incapacitated](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#incapacitated_xphb) condition, the vampire has the [Paralyzed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#paralyzed_xphb) condition until the weapon is removed.
- Sunlight. The vampire takes 20 Radiant damage if it starts its turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, it has [Disadvantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#disadvantage_xphb) on attack rolls and ability checks.
- Immobile Restoration. If the vampire drops to 0 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) while it doesn't have the [Petrified](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#petrified_xphb) condition, it turns to stone, regains 50 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb), and has the [Petrified](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#petrified_xphb) condition for 1 hour.

---
### Actions:
**Multiattack**. The vampire makes two Claw attacks and uses Bite or Guardian's Command.

**Claw**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +9, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 14 (2d8 + 5) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb) condition (escape DC 16) from one of two claws.

**Bite**. _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 16, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or that has the [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb), [Incapacitated](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#incapacitated_xphb), or [Restrained](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_xphb) condition. _Failure:_ 7 (1d4 + 5) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. The target's [Hit Point](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) maximum decreases by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken, and the vampire regains [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) equal to that amount.

**Guardian's Command.** The vampire casts [Compulsion](https://5e.tools/spells.html#compulsion_xphb), requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15).

---
### Bonus actions:
**Resilient Flesh.** _Trigger:_ The vampire is hit by a melee attack roll from an attacker the vampire can see. _Response:_ The vampire reduces the damage it takes from the attack by 11 (2d6 + 4).
