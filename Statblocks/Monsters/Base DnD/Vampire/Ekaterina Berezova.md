### Traits:
**Assassinate**. Ekaterina has advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn't taken a turn in the combat yet. In addition, any hit Ekaterina scores against a creature that is surprised is a critical hit.

**Cunning Action.** Ekaterina can take a bonus action to take the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.

**No Escape.** As a reaction, Ekaterina may make a Chain attack against a creature that moves within 10ft. of her. On ahit, she may pull the creature 5ft. toward her into an unoccupied space and then may make a Bite attack against the creature.

**Regeneration**. Ekaterina regains 10 hit points at the start of her turn if she has at least 1 hit point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If Ekaterina takes radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of her next turn.

**Sneak Attack** (1/Turn). Once per turn, Ekaterina can deal an extra 3d6 damage to one creature she hits with an attack if she has advantage on the attack roll or if another enemy of the target is within 5 feet of it, that enemy isn't incapacitated, and she doesn't have disadvantage on the attack roll.

**Spider Climb.** Ekaterina can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

**Uncanny Dodge.** When a creature Ekaterina can see hits her with an attack, she can use her reaction to halve the attack's damage against her.

**Vampire Weaknesses.** Ekaterina has the following flaws:

- _Forbiddance._ Ekaterina can't enter a residence without an invitation from one of the occupants.
- _Harmed by Running Water._ Ekaterina takes 20 acid damage when she ends her turn in running water.
- _Stake to the Heart._ Ekaterina is destroyed if a piercing weapon made of wood is driven into her heart while she is [incapacitated](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#incapacitated_phb) in her resting place.
- _Sunlight Hypersensitivity._ Ekaterina takes 20 radiant damage when she starts her turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, she has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

### Actions:
**Multiattack**. Ekaterina makes two attacks, only one of which can be a bite attack.

**Chain**. _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage. The target is [grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_phb) (escape DC 16) and pulled 5ft. toward Ekaterina into an unoccupied space. Until this grapple ends, the target is [restrained](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_phb) and takes 7 (2d6) piercing damage at the start of each of its turns. While grappling a creature this way, if Ekaterina targets another creature for a Chain attack, Ekaterina releases the [grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_phb) creature.

**Claws**. _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 8 (2d4 + 3) slashing damage. Instead of dealing damage, Ekaterina can grapple the target (escape DC15).

**Bite**. _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one willing creature, a creature that cannot see Ekaterina, or a creature that is [grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_phb) by Ekaterina, [incapacitated](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#incapacitated_phb), or [restrained](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_phb). _Hit:_ 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage, plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. The target's hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the necrotic damage taken, and Ekaterina regains hit points equal to that amount. The reduction lasts until the target finishes a long rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its hit point maximum to 0.
