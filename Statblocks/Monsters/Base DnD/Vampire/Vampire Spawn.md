### Traits:
Spider Climb. The vampire can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

Vampire Weakness. The vampire has these weaknesses:

- Forbiddance. The vampire can't enter a residence without an invitation from an occupant.
- Running Water. The vampire takes 20 Acid damage if it ends its turn in running water.
- Stake to the Heart. The vampire is destroyed if a weapon that deals Piercing damage is driven into the vampire's heart while the vampire has the [Incapacitated](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#incapacitated_xphb) condition.
- Sunlight. The vampire takes 20 Radiant damage if it starts its turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, it has [Disadvantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#disadvantage_xphb) on attack rolls and ability checks.

---
### Actions:
**Multiattack**. The vampire makes two Claw attacks and uses Bite.

**Claw**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +6, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 8 (2d4 + 3) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb) condition (escape DC 13) from one of two claws.

**Bite**. _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 14, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or that has the [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb), [Incapacitated](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#incapacitated_xphb), or [Restrained](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_xphb) condition. _Failure:_ 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. The target's [Hit Point](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) maximum decreases by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken, and the vampire regains [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) equal to that amount.

---
### Bonus actions:
**Deathless Agility.** The vampire takes the [Dash](https://5e.tools/actions.html#dash_xphb) or [Disengage](https://5e.tools/actions.html#disengage_xphb) action.
