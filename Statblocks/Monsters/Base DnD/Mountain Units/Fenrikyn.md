### Traits:
**Mystical Chain.** The mystical chain attached to the fenrikyn is its greatest weakness and can be severed by an attack dealing 20 Slashing damage against AC 17, which deals no damage to the fenrikyn. If the fenrikyn's chain is destroyed, it takes 18 (4d8) Psychic damage and has the [Stunned](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#stunned_xphb) condition until the end of its next turn. It also loses its Chain Attack action, Whirling Chain action, and Deflecting Chain Reaction.

---
### Actions:
**Multiattack**. The fenrikyn makes one Bite attack and one Chain attack. If its Mystical Chain has been broken, it instead makes two Bite attacks.

**Bite**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage.

**Chain**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +8 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 12 (2d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb) condition (escape DC 16). While grappled, the target cannot move further than 20 feet from the fenrikyn, and its [Speed](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#speed_xphb) is reduced by 10 feet.

**Whirling Chain** (Recharge 6). _Dexterity Saving Throw:_ DC 16 All creatures within 20 feet of the fenrikyn. _Failure:_ 21 (6d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is pushed or pulled 10 feet. _Success:_ Half damage only.

**Deflecting Chain.** _Trigger:_ The fenrikyn is hit by an attack. _Response:_ The fenrikyn adds 3 to its AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.
