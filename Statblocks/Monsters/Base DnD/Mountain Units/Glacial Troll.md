### Traits:
**Frigid Aura.** Nonmagical flames within 15 feet of the troll are snuffed out. Ongoing magical flames, such as those from a [Flaming Sphere](https://5e.tools/spells.html#flaming%20sphere_xphb) or [Wall of Fire](https://5e.tools/spells.html#wall%20of%20fire_xphb) spell, also have a chance of being snuffed out. If any part of such a magical flame overlaps with the aura, the caster must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw. _Failure:_ The spell ends. If the caster succeeds on the save, they do not need to make additional saves for that casting of that spell.

**Icy Carapace.** A creature within 5 feet of the troll that hits it with a melee attack takes 4 (1d8) Cold damage.

**Regeneration.** The glacial troll regains 15 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) at the start of each of its turns. If the glacial troll takes Acid or Fire damage, this trait doesn't function on the glacial troll's next turn. The glacial troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) and doesn't regenerate.

### Actions:
**Multiattack.** The glacial troll makes three Ice Rend attacks. It can substitute one Ice Rend attack with its Hoary Breath.

**Ice Rend.** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 14 (2d8 + 5) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Cold damage.

**Hoary Breath** (Recharge 5–6). _Dexterity Saving Throw:_ DC 16 One target within 5 feet of it. _Failure:_ 14 (3d6) Cold damage, and the target has the [Restrained](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_xphb) condition for 1 minute due to ice forming around it. A creature with the [Restrained](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_xphb) condition can make a DC 16 Strength saving throw at the end of each of its turns to break free from the ice, ending the [Restrained](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_xphb) condition.
