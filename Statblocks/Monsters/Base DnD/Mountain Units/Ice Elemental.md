### Traits:
**Aversion to Fire.** If the elemental takes Fire damage, its Piercing Form trait doesn't function on its next turn, and it has [Disadvantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#disadvantage_xphb) on attack rolls and ability checks until the end of its next turn.

**Piercing Form.** At the start of each of its turns, the elemental deals 5 (1d10) Piercing damage to any creature with 5 feet of it.

---
### Actions:
**Multiattack**. The elemental makes two Ice Claw attacks.

**Ice Claw**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +7, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Cold damage.

**Shard Spray** (Recharge 4–6). _Dexterity Saving Throw:_ DC 14, each creature in a 60-foot [Cone](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#cone%20%5barea%20of%20effect%5d_xphb). _Failure:_ 11 (2d10) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Cold damage, and the creature's [Speed](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#speed_xphb) is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn. _Success:_ Half damage only.
