### Traits:
**Amphibious**. The grendelkyn can breathe air and water.

**Shadow Armor.** The grendelkyn is surrounded by a writhing mantle of shadows. This mantle adds 3 to its AC (included in its stats) and grants it [Advantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#advantage_xphb) on Dexterity (Stealth) checks in [Darkness](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#darkness_xphb) or [Dim Light](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#dim%20light_xphb).

**Sunlight Sensitivity**. While in sunlight, the grendelkyn has [Disadvantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#disadvantage_xphb) on ability checks and attack rolls, and it loses its Shadow Armor. Additionally, it cannot use its Shadow Snare Action, and any existing Shadow Snare melts.

---
### Actions:
**Multiattack.** The grendelkyn makes one Bite and one Claws attack. It can replace its Claws attack with Shadow Snare.

**Bite.** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 12 (2d6 + 5) Piercing damage. On a [Critical Hit](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#critical%20hit_xphb), the target has the [Frightened](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#frightened_xphb) condition until the end of the grendelkyn's next turn.

**Claws**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 10 (1d10 + 5) Slashing damage.

**Shadow Snare.** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 9 (2d8) Cold damage, and the target is wrapped in shadowy tendrils extending from the grendelkyn's body, and the target is subjected to the following effect. _Dexterity Saving Throw:_ DC 14. _Failure:_ The target has the [Blinded](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#blinded_xphb) and [Restrained](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_xphb) conditions. A creature can make a new Dexterity Saving Throw at the end of each of its turns to free itself. Otherwise, it remains ensnared by the tendrils for 1 minute. The Shadow Snare also vanishes if it is exposed to sunlight, the grendelkyn dies or has the [Incapacitated](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#incapacitated_xphb) condition, or it chooses to use Shadow Snare against a new target.

---
### Bonus actions:
**Shadow Port.** A grendelkyn in an area of [Dim Light](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#dim%20light_xphb) or [Darkness](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#darkness_xphb) can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see in [Dim Light](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#dim%20light_xphb) or [Darkness](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#darkness_xphb). If the grendelkyn teleports while it has a creature trapped in a Shadow Snare, that creature also teleports 30 feet, to an unoccupied space within 10 feet of the grendelkyn that is in [Dim Light](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#dim%20light_xphb) or [Darkness](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#darkness_xphb). If no such space is available, the grendelkyn releases the trapped creature when it teleports.

---
### Reactions:
**Frenzied Shriek.** _Trigger:_ The grendelkyn is [Bloodied](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#bloodied_xphb). _Response:_ All creatures within 20 feet of the grendelkyn make a _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 12. _Failure:_ The target has the [Frightened](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#frightened_xphb) condition for 1 minute. A creature affected by the shriek can make a new Wisdom saving throw at the end of each of its turns to end the effect on itself.
