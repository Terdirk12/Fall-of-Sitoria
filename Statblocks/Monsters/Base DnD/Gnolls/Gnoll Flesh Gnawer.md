### Actions:

**Multiattack**. The gnoll makes one Bite attack and two Shortsword attacks.

**Bite**. _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.

**Shortsword**. _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

**Sudden Rush**. Until the end of the turn, the gnoll's speed increases by 60 feet and it doesn't provoke [opportunity attacks](https://5e.tools/actions.html#opportunity%20attack_phb).

---
### Bonus actions:
**Rampage**. After the gnoll reduces a creature to 0 hit points with a melee attack on its turn, the gnoll moves up to half its speed and makes a Bite attack.
