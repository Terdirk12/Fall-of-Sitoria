### Traits:
**Catalyst Infusion**. Until the start of its next turn, the brute's [Speed](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#speed_xphb) increases to 50 feet, it doesn't provoke an [Opportunity Attack](https://5e.tools/actions.html#opportunity%20attack_xphb) when it leaves an enemy's reach, and its [Long Jump](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#long%20jump_xphb) is up to 30 feet and its [High Jump](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#high%20jump_xphb) is up to 15 feet with or without a running start.

**Caustic Secretion**. Until the start of its next turn, its Fist attack deals an extra 11 (2d10) Acid damage (included in the Fist attack below).

**Curse Dependent**. If the brute is targeted by a [Remove Curse](https://5e.tools/spells.html#remove%20curse_xphb) spell or similar magic, until it finishes a [Long Rest](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#long%20rest_xphb), the brute loses its Blindsight, its Withering Eye trait, and its Cursed Gaze action.

**Stim Boost**. Gain 10 [Temporary Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#temporary%20hit%20points_xphb).

**Stimulant Rush**. At the start of its turn, the brute must choose one of the following options (it can't use that option twice in a row):

**Unleashed Eyes** (1/Day). When the brute is [Bloodied](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#bloodied_xphb), its sealed eyes rip open and unleash its curse. It is no longer blind beyond 60 feet, and it can use its Withering Eye trait and Cursed Gaze action until it finishes a [Long Rest](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#long%20rest_xphb), whereupon its eyes seal shut again.

**Withering Eye** (Unleashed Eyes Only). When the brute moves to within 5 feet of a creature, the creature takes 4 (1d8) Psychic damage. A creature takes this damage only once per turn.

---
### Actions:

**Multiattack**. The brute makes two Fist attacks and can use Cursed Gaze if available.

**Fist**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +8, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 9 (1d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. If Caustic Secretion is active, the target takes an extra 11 (2d10) Acid damage.

**Cursed Gaze** (Unleashed Eyes Only). _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 16, each creature in a 30-foot [Cone](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#cone%20%5barea%20of%20effect%5d_xphb). _Failure:_ 9 (2d8) Psychic damage, and the target has the [Frightened](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#frightened_xphb) condition until the start of the brute's next turn. _Success:_ Half damage only.
