### Actions:

**Multiattack**. The skogsra makes three Claw attacks. In place of one Claw attack, it can use Grasping Vines or Spellcasting.

**Claw**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 8 (1d8 + 4) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage.

**Grasping Vines**. _Strength Saving Throw:_ DC 15 One Medium or smaller creature the skogsra can see within 15 feet. _Failure:_ The target is pulled up to 10 feet toward the skogsra and has the [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb) condition (escape DC 15).

**Spellcasting**. The skogsra casts one of the following spells, using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):

- At will: [Friends](https://5e.tools/spells.html#friends_xphb), [Thunderclap](https://5e.tools/spells.html#thunderclap_xphb)
- 1/day each: [Call Lightning](https://5e.tools/spells.html#call%20lightning_xphb), [Detect Thoughts](https://5e.tools/spells.html#detect%20thoughts_xphb), [Disguise Self](https://5e.tools/spells.html#disguise%20self_xphb), [Ice Storm](https://5e.tools/spells.html#ice%20storm_xphb), [Wind Wall](https://5e.tools/spells.html#wind%20wall_xphb)

### Bonus actions:

**Hollow Trap**. The skogsra pulls a creature with the [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb) condition into its hollow body. While held in this way, the target isn't [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb) but has the [Blinded](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#blinded_xphb) and [Restrained](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_xphb) conditions, and it takes 7 (2d6) Slashing damage and 7 (2d6) Poison damage at the start of each of its turns. If this damage reduces a Humanoid creature to 0 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb), that creature is considered [Stable](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#stable_xphb) and has the [Paralyzed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#paralyzed_xphb) condition. This condition lasts for 12 hours or until the [Paralyzed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#paralyzed_xphb) creature regains [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb).

The held creature has [Total Cover](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#cover_xphb) against attacks and other effects outside the skogsra. While holding a creature within its body, the skogsra can't pull another creature into its hollow.

If the skogsra takes 15 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, it must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate that creature, which falls in a space within 5 feet of the skogsra and has the [Prone](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#prone_xphb) condition.

If the skogsra dies, a held creature no longer has the [Restrained](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_xphb) or [Paralyzed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#paralyzed_xphb) condition and can escape from the corpse using 5 feet of movement, exiting with the [Prone](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#prone_xphb) condition.
