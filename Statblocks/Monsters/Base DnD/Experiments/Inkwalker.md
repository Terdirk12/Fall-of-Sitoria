### Traits:
**Adhesive**. The inkwalker adheres to anything that touches it. A Large or smaller creature adhered to the inkwalker has the [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb) condition (escape DC 13). Ability checks made to escape this grapple have [Disadvantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#disadvantage_xphb).

**Ambusher**. In the first round of combat, the inkwalker has [Advantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#advantage_xphb) on attack rolls against any creature it has Surprised.

**Grappler**. The inkwalker has [Advantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#advantage_xphb) on attack rolls against any creature [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb) by it.

**Mimicry**. The inkwalker can mimic sounds it has heard, including voices and animal noises. A creature that hears the sound it makes can tell they are imitations with a successful Wisdom (Insight) check opposed by the inkwalker's Charisma (Deception) check.

**Wall Walker**. The inkwalker can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

### Actions:

**Bite**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Acid damage.

**Pseudopod**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 5 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage, and the target is subjected to Adhesive.

**Shape-Shift**. The inkwalker shape-shifts into a [CR 1/2 or lower beast](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#blankhash,flstsource:,flstchallenge%20rating:min=&0~max=&1/2,flsttype:beast=1,flstmiscellaneous:) Beast or back into its true, amorphous shape. Its game statistics are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying is transformed.
