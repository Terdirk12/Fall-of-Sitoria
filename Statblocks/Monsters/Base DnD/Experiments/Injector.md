### Actions:

**Multiattack**. The injector makes four Syringe attacks. It can replace any number of attacks with uses of Booster Dose instead.

**Syringe**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +10, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 8 (1d4 + 6) Piercing damage, and the target is subjected to one of the following effects (roll 1d4; reroll if the injector has already used that effect during this turn):

- **Lethal Injection**. The target takes 21 (6d6) Poison damage.
- **Numbing Solution**. The target takes 9 (2d8) Cold damage and is subjected to the following effect: _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 16. _Failure:_ The target has the [Frozen](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#frozen_monstersofdrakkenheim) condition (DC 16).
- **Mind Dampener**. The target takes 7 (2d6) Psychic damage and is subjected to the following effect: _Intelligence Saving Throw:_ DC 16. _Failure:_ The target has the [Bewildered](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#bewildered_monstersofdrakkenheim) condition (save ends).
- **Induced Necrosis**. The target takes 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage and is subjected to the following effect: _Strength Saving Throw:_ DC 16. _Failure:_ The target has the [Weakened](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#weakened_monstersofdrakkenheim) condition (save ends).

**Booster Dose**. The injector administers a tincture to a [Friendly](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#friendly%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) creature it can see within 5 feet that isn't a Construct, choosing from the effects below. Once a creature benefits from a tincture, it can't gain that benefit again until it finishes a [Short](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#short%20rest_xphb) or [Long Rest](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#long%20rest_xphb).

**Adrenaline Rush**. The target can use its [Reaction](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#reaction_xphb) to move up to its [Speed](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#speed_xphb) without provoking [Opportunity Attack](https://5e.tools/actions.html#opportunity%20attack_xphb) action or make one weapon attack with [Advantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#advantage_xphb).

**Inoculation**. The injector chooses Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Necrotic, or Poison damage. The target gains [Resistance](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#resistance_xphb) to the chosen damage type for 1 minute.

**Restorative Fluid**. The target regains 35 10d6 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb). If the target is already at its [Hit Point](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) Maximum, it gains these as [Temporary Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#temporary%20hit%20points_xphb) instead.

**Focus**. The target has the [Valor](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#valor_monstersofdrakkenheim) condition 1d4 for 1 minute.

### Reactions:

**Unstable Tank**. _Trigger:_ The injector would take damage. _Response:_ The injector reduces that damage to 0, and one of its tanks violently explodes. The injector chooses one of the effects of its Syringe or Booster Dose. Each creature within 20 feet of it is affected as if the Injector hit it with its Syringe or used Booster Dose on it. The injector can't use that effect until it finishes a [Long Rest](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#long%20rest_xphb).
