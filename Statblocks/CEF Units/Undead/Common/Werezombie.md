### Traits:
**Suppressed Lycanthropy:** If reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb), The Werezombie succumbs to lycanthropy. Their statistics are replaced by those of the [Werewolf](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#werewolf_xmm) stat block, but they retain their alignment; personality; Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores; proficiencies; and languages known. They then immediately revive in their hybrid form with all their [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb).
