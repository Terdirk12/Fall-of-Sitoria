**Damage Immunity**: Fire

**Languages:** Common, Infernal 
**Challenge:** 6

---
### Traits:
**Keen Hearing and Smell:** The hound has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell. 

**Pack Tactics:** The hound has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the hound’s allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn’t Incapacitated. 

**Restrained Existence:** The hound exists in a state of perpetual infernal binding that consumes any mundane bindings that would restrain it as fuel. When it does so, the hound gains a +1 bonus to its Armor Class for the next hour

**Easy kill** if you reduce the hitpoints of this enemy from full to zero with a singular attack; you get +1 (extra)(non-magical) attack.

---
### Actions:
**Multiattack:** The hound makes two Claw attacks then a Bite attack

**Claw:** *Melee Weapon Attack*: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d8 + 3) Slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) Fire damage

**Bite:** *Melee Weapon Attack*: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage and the target must make a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked Prone and become Grappled by the hound on a failure. While a creature is Grappled in this way by the hound, it may only use its bite attack against that creature
