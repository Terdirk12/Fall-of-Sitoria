---
### Traits:
**Magic Resistance.** The huntsman has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Pack’s Protection.** The Armor Class of the huntsman is increased by 2 for each Death Wrought Hound within 15 feet of it (to a maximum of +6). If the huntsman would be affected by an effect that targets an area, it may make one Death Wrought Hound within 10 feet of it the target of that effect in its place (this may result in a single hound being targeted more than once for the same effect).

---
### Actions:
**Multiattack:** The huntsman makes two Infernal Lash attacks or one Infernal Lash and one Bola attack, then uses its Lead ability

**Infernal Lash:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (4d4 + 4) Slashing damage plus 14 (4d6) Fire damage. On a hit, the huntsman may pull the target 10 feet towards them.

**Bola:** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, range 20/60 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (4d4 + 4) Bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or become Restrained. The target or another creature within 5 feet of it may attempt a DC 16 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check as an action, breaking the Restrained creature free on a success. Only two creatures may be Restrained this way at a time due to the amount of bolas carried by the huntsman, and when a creature becomes freed from a bola it vanishes and reappears attached to the huntsman’s belt.

**Lead:** The huntsman spurs on up to 3 Death Wrought Hounds within 15 feet of it via corporal motivation. Until the end of the huntsman’s next turn, the hound’s Speed increases by 10 feet, and it gains a +2 bonus to attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws it makes.


---
### Reactions 

**Rebuking Command.** After the huntsman is dealt damage from a hostile creature, it may command a Death Wrought Hound to protect it. The chosen hound uses its Reaction to move up to its Speed, attempting to end that movement as close to the huntsman as possible.
