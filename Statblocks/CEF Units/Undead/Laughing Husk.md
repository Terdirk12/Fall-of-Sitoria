### Traits:
**Mock Death:** The first time the Laughing Husk is reduced to **half HP or fewer**, it immediately collapses, falls prone, and becomes incapacitated until the start of its next turn, appearing dead.
If its **head is destroyed or removed** during this time, it dies permanently.

**Second Breath:** At the start of its next turn after Mock Death, if its head remains intact, the Laughing Husk stands up and resumes combat with its remaining HP.

**Vengeful Focus:** When Second Breath triggers, choose the creature that reduced the Husk to Mock Death. Until either creature is reduced to 0 HP:

- The Husk has advantage on attack rolls against that creature
- The Husk cannot willingly target other creatures
- The Husk ignores opportunity attacks from creatures other than its focus
- The Husk moves recklessly to reach its focus
