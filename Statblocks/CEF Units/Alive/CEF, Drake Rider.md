### Traits:
**Draconic Essence.** Choose one damage type: acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison. The drake rider has resistance to that damage type. The bite attack deals an extra 2 (1d4) damage of the chosen type.

**Heavy Cavalry Discipline.** The drake rider has advantage on saving throws against being frightened while within 10 feet of an allied drake rider, man-at-arms, footman, or a friendly officer.

**Steady Mount.** The drake rider has advantage on ability checks and saving throws to resist being knocked prone. If an effect would dismount the rider, it instead fails unless the drake rider is incapacitated.

---
### Actions:
**Multiattack.** The drake rider makes two attacks: one with its **warhammer** and one with its **bite**.

**Warhammer.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
_Hit:_ 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

**Bite.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
_Hit:_ 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) damage of the draconic essence.

**Javelin.** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target.  
_Hit:_ 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.
