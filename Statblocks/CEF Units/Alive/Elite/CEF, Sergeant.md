### Traits:
**Commanding Presence.** Allied guards, footmen, man-at-arms, trackers, and arbalists within 10 feet of the sergeant who can see or hear it gain a **+1 bonus to attack rolls** and have **advantage on saving throws against being frightened**.

**Steady the Line.** While the sergeant is within 5 feet of an allied footman or man-at-arms who isn’t incapacitated, the sergeant gains a +1 bonus to AC.

---
### Actions:
**Multiattack.** The sergeant makes two melee attacks.

**Longsword.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
_Hit:_ 6 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage.

**Light Crossbow.** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +3 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target.  
_Hit:_ 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.

**Commanding Shout (Recharge 5–6).** One ally the sergeant can see within 30 feet that can hear it can use its reaction to **move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks** or **make one weapon attack**.

---
### Reaction
**Parry.** The sergeant adds 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the sergeant must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
