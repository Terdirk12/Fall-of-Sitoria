### Traits:
**Disciplined:** Advantage on saving throws against being frightened whilst within 10 feet of allied arbalists or commander.

**Volley Doctrine:** If the arbalist hasn’t moved this turn and is within 10 feet of an allied footman or man-at-arms who isn’t incapacitated, the arbalist gains a +1 bonus to ranged attack rolls.

**Alchemical Munitions (3/Day):** When the arbalist hits with its arbalest, it can apply one of the following effects:

- **Incendiary Flask:** The attack deals an extra 3 (1d6) fire damage. The target also takes 2 (1d4) fire damage at the start of its next turn unless it or another creature uses an action to smother the flames.

- **Toxic Flask:** The attack deals an extra 2 (1d4) poison damage, and the target must succeed on a **DC 12** Constitution saving throw or be **poisoned** until the end of its next turn.
