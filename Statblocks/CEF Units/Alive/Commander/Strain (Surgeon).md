### Traits:
**Disciplined:** Advantage on saving throws against being frightened whilst within 10 feet of allied chirurgeons, sergeant, or commander.

**Triage Training:** The chirurgeon can move through the space of allied creatures. (It still can’t end its turn there.)

---
### Actions:
**Shortsword.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
_Hit:_ 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage.

**Battlefield Medicine (3/Day).** The chirurgeon chooses one creature within 5 feet. The target:
- regains **6 (1d6 + 3)** hit points, **or**
- if it has 0 hit points, it instead regains **1 hit point**.

**Stabilize.** The chirurgeon stabilizes one creature within 5 feet that has 0 hit points.

---
### Bonus Actions:
**Battle Command.** Choose one creature within 30 feet of Strain that Strain can see. If the chosen creature can see or hear Strain, that creature can use its reaction to make one melee attack or to take the [Dodge](https://5e.tools/actions.html#dodge_phb) or [Hide](https://5e.tools/actions.html#hide_phb) action.
