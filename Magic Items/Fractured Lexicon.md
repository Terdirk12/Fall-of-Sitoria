**Fractured Lexicon**
Contains the following 6 spells:
- Magic Missile 
- Fireball
- Fly
- Shield
- Hold Person
- Enlarge/Reduce


Once per long rest can cast a spell from the Lexicon, without the use of a spellslot:
A spellslot can be used to use the spells in the spellbook at a higher level. Example, use a lvl 2 spellslot & the Lexicon to cast a lvl 2 Lexicon magic missile.
when doing so roll 1d6
1-3 The Spell gains an effect:
4-6 Normal cast

Carrying the Lexicon gives +1 to spell attack rolls.

*Special Effects for every spell:*

*Magic Missile*
d6
1: The missile behave erratically and split into multiple smaller missiles each carrying half the damage of the whole missile. You may retarget them.
2: The missile explodes Infront of the target into a acid wave, deals +1d4 acid damage per missile
3: The missile freezes mid-air and starts flickering, on contact they explode and next turn they continue their path to their original target dealing +1d8 damage.
4: The missile explodes Infront of the target into a Fiery cloud, deals +1d4 fire damage per missile in a 5ft diameter sphere.
5: The missile explodes Infront of the target into a icy blast, deals +1d4 cold damage per missile and half targets movement speed.
6: The turns into a pointy spike, penetrating the first target and heading for a second target, nearest to the first.

*Fireball*
d4
1: Remove the AOE effect of the fireball, instead take all damage that would have been done by the AOE and cast it on the target closest to the point of origin
2: Remove the AOE effect of the fireball, instead take all damage that would have been done by the AOE and cast it on the target closest to the point of origin
3: Double the AOE Range but half the damage.
4: Double the AOE Range but half the damage.

*Fly*
d4
1: Gain twice the flight speed.
2: The flight effect is given in an 10ft AOE.
3: Instead of flight, get Feather falling and quadruple jump distance.
4: Half the flight speed.

*Shield*
d4
1: Shield Persists an extra turn.
2: Shield gives an extra +5 AC.
3: Shield is also given to the nearest ally.
4: Shield crumbles after the first hit.

*Hold Person*
d4
1: Held person slowly levitates into the air, 10ft per turn.
2: Held person sinks into the ground up to their middle.
3: Held person also drops weapons.
4: Held person has uncanny movement, making them dodge attacks.

*Enlarge/Reduce*
d4
1: Enlarges/reduces targets arms, giving them +2/-2 to melee attacks.
2: Enlarges/reduces targets legs, giving additional +20ft/-20ft Speed.
3: Enlarges/reduces targets heart, giving an +1/-1 action per turn, but also takes 1d8 true damage per turn.
4: Enlarges/reduces targets weapon, giving +2/-2 to hit for melee attacks.
