CEF Ambush, a quick way to kill off [[Igina]], also first encounter with [[Capitol Elite Forces, CEF]]

"Whilst walking you hear a loud thud and then a loud chunk, when you look at where the sound came from you see Igina's body pierced by a large Ballista bolt to a tree. Her arms limp, her head hanging and blood drooling from the mouth. You duck to cover and try to figure out where the bolt came from, throw for Perception, DC 8."
