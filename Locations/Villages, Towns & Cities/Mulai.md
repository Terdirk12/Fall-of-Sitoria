people seem happy even if the village seems a bit dreary... they are all loyal to count Voromir. the village will turn hostile if/when the players attack the Voromirs.

Upon arrival the villagers send someone to the count to make him alert to the parties presence. and a villager remarks that if they waited for 2 weeks they wouldnt have been caught in the storm.


There is a farm to the south of Mulai with a barn somewhat hidden in the forest. the barn is locked tight, there are no windows and the door seems sealed shut with some sort of wax. according to the farmer there are cows inside, keeping warm in the cold weather. The wax is there as sealant against the cold.

These 'cows' are humans used as bloodbags. Upon seeing or freeing one of the 'cows' the entire village will become hostile.
