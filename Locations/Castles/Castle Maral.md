## The Banquet

The Voromirs will invite the players to a banquet where the count will discuss his options with the players, the count will be reluctant to lend out his castle to the rebellion but will offer the party gold in return for dealing with a pest in his basement. 1500 gold.

## The Deal

At a certain point the count will talk in private with each hero and offer them a boon in return for something small: wearing a necklace with a dull red gen that they cant take off.

He will offer to each hero to turn them into a vampire but also will offer a small personal boon to each hero

Peter: Sanguine Drain
Otto: Access to the library with long forbidden books
Gek: The wealth of a king
Taylin: An animal form more powerful then any in their repertoire
#### Player Characters as Vampires

The game statistics of a player character transformed into a vampire spawn and then a vampire don't change, except that the character's Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution scores become 18 if they aren't higher. In addition, the character gains the vampire's damage resistances, darkvision, traits, and actions. Attack and damage rolls for the vampire's attacks are based on Strength.

The save DC for Charm is 8 + the vampire's proficiency bonus + the vampire's Charisma modifier. The character's alignment becomes lawful evil, and the DM might take control of the character until the vampirism is reversed with a [wish](https://5e.tools/spells.html#wish_phb) spell or the character is killed and brought back to life.

## The Aftermath
If one deal was accepted the count will stay with his initial deal

If all deals were denied he will fake going for the initial deal and then ambush the players. surrounding them with his entire court.

He will imprison them and contact the imperial army. who will come to pick them up for a grand execution in the capital, but until then he will play with his guests in the form of a tournament
