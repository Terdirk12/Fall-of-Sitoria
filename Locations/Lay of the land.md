To get and idea of the lay of the land of [[sitoria]]: see [[Roads.canvas|Roads]]

In the South-East lies a medium Town called [[Mosswood]]. the towns outskirts contain a variety of farms such as [[Outermoss Farm]].

To the North-East of [[Mosswood]] lies a forest and within that forest is [[Darkwood]], a small village known for its spider silk. [[Darkwood]] contains a spider farm called [[The Silk Loom]]

To the North of [[Mosswood]] and North-West of [[Darkwood]] lies the Broken Mountains, a smaller mountain range that is very broken up often looking like a couple of lone mountains instead of a larger range. within the broken mountains are various caves home to goblins like the [[Broken Hand Clan]] and other creatures.

To the west of [[Mosswood]] streams a medium-large river called the [[Helte]]. this river goes all the way from the south of sitoria to the north-west outside of the lands of [[Sitoria]] where it reaches the sea. during its journey it streams along [[The High Peaks]], a tall mountain range, central in [[Sitoria]], it passes along the capitol of sitoria and when exiting sitoria it goes by [[Eldian's Temple]].
