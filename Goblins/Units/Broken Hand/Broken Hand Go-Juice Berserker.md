Medium humanoid (goblinoid), chaotic evil

Armor Class: 15 (studded leather + natural agility)  
Hit Points: 38 (7d8 + 7)  
Speed: 30 ft

STR 16 (+3) | DEX 14 (+2) | CON 12 (+1) | INT 8 (-1) | WIS 10 (+0) | CHA 8 (-1)

Saving Throws: STR +5, DEX +4  
Skills: Perception +2  
Senses: Darkvision 60 ft, passive Perception 12  
Languages: Goblin

---

### Traits

**Go-Juice (Ingested):** For 1 minute after drinking:

First melee attack per turn has advantage.  
First melee attack against it each round also has advantage.  
At the start of its turn, roll d6 — on 1, take 1d4 psychic damage.  
  

**Go-Juice (Injected/Applied):** For 1 minute:

Movement +10 ft.  
First melee attack per turn has advantage.

At the start of its turn, takes 1d6 true damage.  
  

Reckless Charge (1/Turn): The berserker can move up to its speed toward a creature and make one melee attack with advantage.

---

### Actions

*Multiattack. The berserker makes two attacks with its crude axe.*

**Crude Axe.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d8+3 slashing damage.

**Throwing Spear.** Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, range 20/60 ft., one target. Hit: 1d6+3 piercing damage.
