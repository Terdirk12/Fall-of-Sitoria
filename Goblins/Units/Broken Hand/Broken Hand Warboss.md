### Traits

Incensed Greatmace.  
This massive two-handed mace has a spiked head with an incenser welded on. Whenever the Warboss hits a creature with this weapon, the Go-Juice fumes erupt.

- Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
- Hit: 2d8 + 3 bludgeoning damage.  
- Special Effect: Target must succeed a DC 13 Con save or suffer Go-Juice Mist (see below).  

Go-Juice Mist (Aura, 10 ft).  
At the start of each of the Warboss’s turns, all creatures within 10 ft make a DC 13 Con save.

- On fail: gain +2 melee damage until start of next turn, but take 1d4 true damage at start of their turn.  
- Goblins automatically succeed (they’ve been “trained”).  

Nimble Commander.  
As a bonus action, the Warboss can command a goblin within 30 ft to make a weapon attack.

Reckless Surge (1/rest).  
When reduced below half HP, the Warboss smashes the incenser into the ground. All creatures within 15 ft immediately make a DC 13 Con save or become “Juiced” (advantage on melee attacks, but they take 1d6 true damage at start of their turn).
