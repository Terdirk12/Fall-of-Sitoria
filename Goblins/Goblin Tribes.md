So goblins are an ever present threat in dnd and so they will be in this campaign, there will be multiple goblin clans each on a sort of neutral-enemy state with the others, they do attack each other on sight but don't go out of their way to attack others since they are so busy raiding [[Sitoria]].

The players can fight these goblins forever but in the case they want to “Fix” the goblin problem, they will need to start a goblin war, forcing clan versus clan in order to make the goblins so busy fighting each other that normal rebellion groups can clear out all remaining goblins in [[Sitoria]].  
  
The players can invoke wars by doing:
- False-flag raids, Steal goods from clan A and dump them at clan B
- Assassinating leaders, Kill a goblin leader and plant evidence of another clan
- Burning goblin goods, Sabotaging Go-Juice production and blame other clans
- Empowering lower-ranked clans, Making smaller clans stronger so they can be a threat to bigger clans.


This will EVENTUALLY lead to a singular big goblin coalition, one goblin uniting multiple clans, this could be an end-game threat for if the party ever runs out of content.
