"The final day of joy before the harsh Sitorian winter sets in"

Herstog is a Sitorian national day of celebration, in the cities communities host eating contests and decorate their streets. whilst in the countryside they dance, sing, drink, eat and more

**Urban Herstog.** 
During Herstog commoners in the cities colour their streets in vibrant colours, to add some colour to the otherwise gray streets. Nobles host feasts to reward their workers and even the poor are not forgotten. Merchants at the market sell Herstog merchandise like flower crowns and colourfull fruits. And often a parade is held where people use flowery carts to make their streets smell sweet. 

**Herstog in the countryside.** 
During Herstog the countryside of Sitoria is filled with festivities and music. Farmers hold special markets where they sell silias a special flower that blooms vibrant colours based on where it bloomed. They sing folk songs and form musical bands, they dance around flower hedges trimmed into various flowery shapes and tell tales of old to each other. There are drinking contests where the winner gets a barrel of nectarwine and big long tables with banquets of food where all are welcome to join. 

**General Herstog.** 
Herstog food is often colorful and sweet filled with aroma and juices, throughout the whole festival sweet scents can be smelled. 
Herstog clothing is often everyday clothing adorned with flowers in all shapes and sizes, colorful and flowy.
